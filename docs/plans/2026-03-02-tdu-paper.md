# Targeted Direction Unlearning (TDU) — Research Paper Plan

> **For executor:** Use sessions_spawn to dispatch each major section. Each agent should produce outputs to specific paths.

**Goal:** Write a complete academic paper demonstrating TDU as a novel, effective, and interpretable method for targeted knowledge unlearning in LLMs.

**Key Contribution:** Unlike gradient-based unlearning methods, TDU discovers interpretable directions that encode specific facts, then surgically removes them via rank-one projection. This provides both effectiveness AND interpretability.

**Target Venue:** NeurIPS 2025 / ICML 2025 style (8-10 pages + appendix)

## Key Hypotheses to Test

1. **Layer specificity:** Facts are primarily encoded in early-middle layers (layers 1-4 in GPT-2/Pythia)
2. **Direction interpretability:** Directions for related facts have high cosine similarity
3. **Surgical precision:** TDU achieves high efficacy with minimal retain set damage
4. **Efficiency:** TDU is faster than gradient-based methods (no iterative optimization)
